7. BUTTONS

8. CERTIFICATIONS

* Add a small staggered reveal animation when certification cards become visible.

9. REDUCED MOTION

* Respect the user's `prefers-reduced-motion` accessibility setting.
* If reduced motion is enabled, disable or minimize animations.

10. PERFORMANCE

* Keep animations lightweight and smooth.
* Prefer CSS transitions/animations and Intersection Observer.
